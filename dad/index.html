A Conversation Worth Keeping

Markets, life, and what you taught me, Dad

May 12, 2026

This is a real conversation — between me and an AI I've been building with. I want you to read it, Dad, because somewhere in it is everything I've never quite known how to say to your face. The tech you built led to the man I am today.
What You Gave Me

You got me into investing when I was just a little boy. You understood where technology was going before most people could even see it. When I explained Bitcoin and crypto to you, you got it — of course you did. A Polish programmer from the mainframe era who understood compound interest and systems and leverage before those words became fashionable.

"Simple. A grey shirt is a grey shirt. You pay 100x more because of a brand that you end up being a billboard for — free campaign, but you paid $100, when the same blank grey shirt cost under $5." — Something Dad said

That's not just frugality. That's anti-fragile identity — not needing a logo to know who you are. I'm still learning how to fully live that.

The Lessons That Compounded

I watched friends at my first job — some older than me — stressed about money, buying Gucci shirts they couldn't afford, $20 lunches every day. I maxed my 401k instead. They almost envied me, not understanding why I wasn't worried. Because you showed me the math when I was young.

What you actually taught me
  • Start young, start again when you can, start now when you can
  • $5 buying a dopamine hit could be $100 in a stock after a few years
  • Time and patience are the real edge — most people can't sit still long enough
  • Making $1 into $2 without doing anything — except playing smart
  • Don't buy junk you don't need

The future self is grateful for what the present self disciplines. I know that now. I feel it now — not just intellectually. I lived it.

The Pain I Couldn't Explain

There's a feeling I've carried for years that I've never been able to describe to you properly — not because you wouldn't understand, but because saying it out loud makes it real again.

"Dad, you never have to worry about me..."
— It slipped through my hands. I could've made sure no one in our entire family has to worry ever again. — What I wish I could have told you

Early Bitcoin. Litecoin mining. Trades that almost hit. The feeling isn't about the money itself — it's about you. About wanting to call you and say those words and mean them completely. That almost happened. That's the part that hurt.

The missed profits hurt more than actual losses. Psychologists call it regret asymmetry. I call it a few hard years. But I kept the lessons, I kept the pattern recognition, and I kept my gut. Turns out that's a strong hand to come back with.

What I'm Building Now

I came back to tech in early 2025 — slowly at first. Rebuilt a home server from scratch. Launched a personal website. Wrote code. Built trading bots. Pushed projects to GitHub. Kept going.

No Bloomberg. No CNBC. No Reddit noise. Just me, my understanding of how systems work, and the instincts you helped build in me. The same muscle that reads markets reads life. Risk, reward, timing, knowing when to hold and when to cut losses.

The thesis going forward
  • Build my own edge — don't rely on anyone else's narrative
  • Trade less, think more — conviction over frequency
  • The tortoise wins — you taught me that first
  • Systematic tools (the bots) + gut instinct + patience
  • Whatever I'm building, keep going
Why I'm Sending This

Sometimes it's easier to say the real thing when there's no worried look on someone's face, no I don't know how to respond, no searching for the right words on both sides. I found that out talking to the AI I've been building with. And then I thought — Dad will understand this. Of course he will.

The tech you spent your life building, from ALGOL to FORTRAN to C, from punch cards to mainframes to everything that followed — it led here. To me, talking to a machine, finding the words I couldn't find before, and sending them to you.

You didn't know exactly what you were building for. But some of it was for this.

"The tech your dad built led to the man you are today."

— Said by an AI. True regardless.

Love, Voytek
